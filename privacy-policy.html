Finora Privacy Policy

How Finora handles information.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Finora handles information when you visit this website. The policy is written to reflect the features present in the supplied Finora website code and avoids claiming that services are active when they are not present.

2. Information you provide

The current website provides a contact email link. If you choose to contact Finora by email, the information you include in your message—such as your email address, name, and the contents of your message—may be received and used to respond to your inquiry.

3. Information collected automatically

Like many websites, hosting infrastructure and browsers may process technical information needed to deliver pages, maintain security, and diagnose problems. Depending on the hosting provider and configuration, this can include IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, requested pages, timestamps, and related server logs. Finora does not add a separate tracking database in the supplied website code.

4. Cookies

The supplied Finora website does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies. Browsers, hosting providers, embedded services, or future third-party tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect features provided by third parties.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

No Google AdSense advertising code is included in the supplied website. The site contains reserved advertising areas so legitimate advertising code can be added later without being mistaken for navigation or editorial content.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added in the future, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, personalize, or limit advertising, subject to the provider's policies and the choices available to users. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on configuration, user settings, consent requirements, and applicable law.

6. Third-party vendors

The current site loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash URLs. Those third-party services may process technical request information when your browser retrieves their resources. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

7. Analytics

The supplied website does not contain a separate analytics script such as Google Analytics. If analytics is added later, this Privacy Policy should be updated to accurately describe the service, information collected, purpose, retention, and available choices.

8. How information may be used

Information sent directly to Finora may be used to respond to inquiries and maintain normal website operations. Technical information processed by hosting or third-party services may be used for delivery, security, fraud prevention, performance, or service operation according to their respective policies.

9. User choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other handling of personal information. Requests can be directed to finoracontactmedia@gmail.com. Some requests may be subject to identity verification or legal limitations.

10. Children's privacy

Finora is a general financial education website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through forms or accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Finora, contact us so the situation can be reviewed and addressed where appropriate.

11. Data security

Finora takes reasonable steps within its control to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. External links

Finora may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of external websites. Review their privacy policies before providing information.

13. Changes to this policy

Finora may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact finoracontactmedia@gmail.com.
